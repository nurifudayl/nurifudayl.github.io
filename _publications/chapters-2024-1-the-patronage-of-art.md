## About the Chapter

This chapter examines art patronage as a historically changing relationship shaped by unequal access to resources, reciprocal obligations, and political authority. It traces the shift from feudal patronage to patrimonial systems and, later, to forms of support associated with the bourgeoisie and the industrial era.

The chapter also considers the Ottoman context, where sultans and state institutions supported artists through offices, status, rewards, and educational opportunities. It explores how these arrangements connected artistic production to political legitimacy and the changing place of artists within the bureaucracy.

## Contribution

- Traces the changing relationship between patrons and artists across different social and political systems.
- Distinguishes feudal, patrimonial, and industrial forms of patronage.
- Examines the role of Ottoman rulers and institutions in supporting artistic production.
